Know your customer (KYC) and customer due diligence

We confirm who you are before you can move money. Business accounts are checked at a higher level than personal ones. If we cannot confirm who you are, or something does not add up, we may limit or pause an account until it does, and we will tell you what we need.

Anti-money laundering and sanctions

We monitor activity for signs of money laundering, fraud and terrorist financing. We screen customers and payments against sanctions lists, and we report suspicious activity to the authorities where the law requires it. We may be legally unable to tell you when we have done so.

Data protection

We collect only what we need to run your account and meet legal duties. Your personal data is handled under the data protection laws of the countries we serve. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it where the law allows.

Access control and sign-in protection

Only people who need access to customer data to do their job can see it, and that access is logged. We protect sign-in with a password plus a second check, and we will never ask you for your PIN, password or one-time code.

Risk and incident management

We watch for outages, fraud and security incidents all day. When an incident affects customers, we fix it first, then tell you what happened, what it affected and what we changed. Live service status is on our status page.

Refunds and disputes

If a payment went wrong, tell us as soon as you can. We look into it, keep you updated, and refund what is owed. Where a payment partner or a bank is involved, we work with them on your behalf.

Record keeping

We keep transaction and identity records for the period the law requires, so that we can answer your questions, resolve disputes and cooperate with regulators.
