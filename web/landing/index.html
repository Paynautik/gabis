Questions, answered

What is gabis?

gabis is an AI agent for collecting money. It handles payments, receipts, refunds, installments and reconciliation in one place, with stablecoin settlement across borders.

Which countries does it work in?

Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Uganda, Tanzania, South Sudan and Liberia, with more countries coming soon.

Which currencies can I hold?

One account holds 12 currency wallets, including naira, shillings, cedis, US dollars, British pounds and euros.

How does invoicing work?

Describe what you are owed in a single sentence. gabis drafts the invoice for you to review, then sends it and follows up on it.

What is stablecoin settlement?

Cross-border payments settle in stablecoins, around the clock, so you are not waiting on bank opening hours.

How does gabis protect my money?

gabis checks transfers for fraud and asks you to confirm anything unusual before money moves.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the payment method and the country. Open an account to see exactly what applies before you pay anything.
